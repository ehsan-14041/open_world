# Harness vs narrator: four blind comparisons (November 2026)

Results and reading guide: [`reports/harness_vs_chat/RESULTS.md`](../../reports/harness_vs_chat/RESULTS.md).

> **Notice: the "private" parts of the actor files are fiction.**
> The scenarios in `v3/` use real, named governments and companies with a hypothetical trigger. Every
> "private" / «پنهان» section (red lines, internal numbers, undeclared intentions, private messages) was
> invented by a language model for the exercise. They are not reports, leaks or claims about the real
> actors, and nothing in `v3/`, `pairs_v3/`, `pairs_v4_raw/` or `pairs_v4_bg/` should be read as fact.
> Some figures in the "public" parts came from the writers' memory or weak sources and were not verified.

| Round | Protocol (written before the outputs) | Question | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `PROTOCOL.md` | original engine, one call per seat, vs one plain answer | harness preferred 3 of 12 |

The follow-up accuracy check on resolved questions is in `research/forecast_test/PROTOCOL_DOSSIER.md`.

All outputs were produced by Claude sub-agents of a coding session (seats, referees and narrators: Sonnet;
scenario writers and judges: Opus). Judges are language models; they scored how outputs read, not whether
they are correct.
