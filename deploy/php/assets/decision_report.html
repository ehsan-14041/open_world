Limitations

What this does — and doesn't do

This does

  • Compare three decisions under the same assumptions.
  • Show how the result changes when an important assumption changes.
  • Make every assumption behind the comparison visible.

This does not

  • Predict the future or guarantee an outcome.
  • Model how competitors react.
  • Model the wider economy, seasons or new customers.
  • Know your customers' price sensitivity — unless you bring evidence.
